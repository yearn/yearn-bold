# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Liquity V2 users arriving from the Liquity ecosystem who hold BOLD and want a
simple, hands-off way to earn Stability Pool yield while keeping their position
denominated in BOLD.

## Product Purpose

The yBOLD lander explains how Yearn allocates BOLD across Liquity V2 Stability
Pools and gives visitors a direct path to deposit, monitor, and withdraw. It
should build confidence quickly, make realized returns and fees legible, and
preserve the complete deposit and withdrawal workflow on one page.

## Brand Personality

Direct, mechanical, and reassuring. The experience should feel clearly Yearn
while remaining familiar to Liquity and BOLD users.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, glossy fintech treatments, gradients, heavy
shadows, glass effects, excessive decoration, and layouts that obscure the
deposit action or strategy mechanics.

## Design Principles

- Make the BOLD-in, BOLD-out value proposition immediately clear.
- Lead with live, verifiable product facts rather than promotional claims.
- Explain automation and risk in short, concrete language.
- Use Yearn identity as the anchor and Liquity cues as supporting context.
- Keep the deposit and withdrawal workflow accessible and visually dominant.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, strong
contrast, large touch targets, and responsive layouts without horizontal page
scrolling. Do not rely on color alone to communicate status or meaning.
